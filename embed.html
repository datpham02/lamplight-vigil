Lamplight Vigil — embedding harness

Not the game. This page exists so the GAM-41 acceptance criteria can be checked by hand: two games on one page, in containers smaller than the window, next to an ordinary text field. Nothing here is imported by the game — it only calls mount().

  1. Mount both. Destroy one: the other keeps running.
  2. Click in the text field and type r and shift: neither game reacts. Click a game and type: the text field stays empty.
  3. AudioContexts created stays at 0 until you first click or press a key inside a game.
  4. Each game letterboxes inside its box and never paints onto the checked background.
  5. Start a run, click away to another window, come back: the clock has not moved and the game says so.
  6. Mount/destroy ×20 then take a heap snapshot — listeners, timers and audio nodes must not grow.
